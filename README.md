# Guardian Research Portfolio

Independent research portfolio focused on digital assets, macroeconomics, regulation, market infrastructure and institutional adoption.

## About the project

Since 2024, I have independently developed Guardian, a structured research and intelligence framework for analysing the evolution of digital-asset markets and financial infrastructure.

The work includes approximately 10 hours of daily research, source verification, pattern recognition, scenario analysis and continuous model development.

## Research areas

- Digital assets and institutional adoption
- Tokenization and market infrastructure
- Cross-border payments and settlement
- Custody, collateral and liquidity
- Macroeconomics and global financial conditions
- Regulation in the United States, Europe and Switzerland
- Central banks, sovereign debt and monetary policy

## Methodology

Guardian combines:

- Primary-source research
- Source-quality classification
- Signal deduplication
- Structured evidence mapping
- Counter-signal analysis
- Scenario testing
- Falsification criteria
- Pattern recognition across independent institutions and markets

The complete Guardian architecture is proprietary. This public portfolio presents a professional overview of the methodology without disclosing sensitive or confidential components.
